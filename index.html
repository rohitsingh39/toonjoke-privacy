Privacy Policy for ToonJoke Studio

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 • Developer: Rohit Kumar Singh

This Privacy Policy explains how ToonJoke Studio ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information when you use our mobile application and web studio.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: If you sign in using Email or Google Sign-In (powered by Supabase Authentication), we collect your name, email address, and unique account identifier to sync your profile and project drafts.
  • Microphone & Audio Recordings: When you use the voice-over recording feature, the App accesses your device microphone solely to record audio clips for character lip-sync and video export. Audio is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to external servers unless you explicitly save or share your exported video.
  • Animation Projects & Media: Custom backgrounds, character poses, and exported MP4 videos are stored locally on your device storage.
  • Bug Reports & Diagnostics: If you voluntarily submit an in-app bug report, we collect your report description, email address (if signed in), basic device metadata (platform, screen resolution), and current scene statistics to diagnose and fix issues.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and save your animation drafts.
  • To enable voice recording, sound effects, and MP4 video rendering on your device.
  • To respond to feedback and resolve technical bugs reported during testing and use.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertisers. The App uses Supabase (for secure authentication and bug report storage) and Google OAuth (if you choose Google Sign-In), both of which encrypt data in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete local projects and recordings anytime by clearing app data or uninstalling the App. To request permanent deletion of your account profile or submitted bug reports from our cloud database, email kingrohit20177@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request — ToonJoke Studio", and your data will be deleted within 7 days.

5. Children's Privacy

ToonJoke Studio is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
